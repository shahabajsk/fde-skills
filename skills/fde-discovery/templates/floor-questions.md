# Floor questions

Ask these to the people who do the work, at their desk, with a live case open. Cut to the 15 that matter. Write down the answer and the example case that goes with it.

## The work

1. Walk me through the last case you closed. What did you open first?
2. Which cases do you dread? What makes them hard?
3. What do you check that the SOP does not tell you to check?
4. When do you break the SOP, and who would agree you were right to?
5. How do you know a case is finished?

## Decisions

6. Which decisions do you make alone, and which do you take to someone?
7. Show me a case where two of you would decide differently.
8. What information do you wish you had at the moment you decide?

## Errors

9. What is the most expensive mistake someone here has made? How was it caught?
10. Which mistakes are found by the customer before you find them?
11. What can you undo, and what can you not undo?

## Systems

12. How many screens do you use per case? Where do you copy and paste?
13. Which system is wrong most often, and how do you work around it?
14. What happens when a system is down?

## Volume and timing

15. What does the worst day of the year look like? What breaks first?
16. Which cases take ten times longer than normal?

## Escalation

17. Who do you escalate to, how, and how long until you hear back?
18. Which escalations turn out to be unnecessary?

## Change

19. If a tool did half of this for you, which half would you hand over first? Which half would you never hand over?
20. What would make you stop trusting that tool?
